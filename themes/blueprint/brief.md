## Aesthetic — Blueprint
A drafting table: deep blueprint-blue ground (#0f2a4a), paper-white foreground (#eef4f8), line work in translucent white, and a single red-pencil accent (#ff5d40) reserved for markers, "you are here" indicators, and the one thing the reader must see; monochrome otherwise. Fraunces (display serif, italic red-pencil accents), Geist (body), Geist Mono (uppercase wide-tracked micro-labels + numerals). Fine graph-paper grid instead of dots; technical-drawing motifs welcome — hairline dimension lines with end ticks, hatched regions, small-caps annotations pinned to leader lines. Fonts are already provided — do NOT @import. Avoid AI-slop (no Inter/Roboto/system-ui, no gradients, no rounded-card grids, no clip-art).
